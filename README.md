Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[Till Firmware Do Us Part](https://make.fun/c/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929).

A robot's wedding vow in one sentence (147 characters, under the 200 limit): funny and heartfelt at once, in a machine's own voice — updates, crashes, low batteries, and the one bug it refuses to patch is its beloved.

`main.py` (Python 3) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I promise to love you through every update, every crash, and every low battery, because my favourite bug was always you — and I refuse to patch it.

Run it locally with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
